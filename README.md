# Vendor Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive data-driven analysis of procurement and sales performance. By leveraging procurement data, sales volume, and profit margin distributions, this dashboard enables strategic decision-making to optimize inventory, mitigate supply chain bottlenecks, and maximize gross profit.

## Key Objectives

* 
**Profitability Optimization:** Identifying high-margin, low-volume products to implement targeted promotional bundling or price elasticity adjustments.


* 
**Supply Chain Risk Management:** Analyzing vendor purchase contributions to identify dependencies, specifically addressing the risk of stock-outs from primary vendors like Diageo North America INC.


* 
**Inventory Efficiency:** Utilizing statistical insights to drive an "optimal purchase volume" policy, aiming to balance bulk discounts against storage and holding costs.



## Dashboard Insights

The Executive Dashboard provides a unified view of key performance indicators (KPIs):

* **Total Sales:** $441.41M
* **Total Purchases:** $307.34M
* **Gross Profit:** $134.07M
* **Profit Margin:** 38.72%
* **Unsold Capital:** $2.71M

## Strategic Rationales

* 
**Top Performers:** We track "Top 10 Vendors" and "Top Selling Brands" (e.g., Jack Daniels No 7 Black) to protect core revenue streams and negotiate better long-term pricing contracts.


* 
**Statistical Certainty:** By calculating 95% Confidence Intervals for profit margins, leadership can differentiate between real structural performance shifts and random data noise.


* 
**Promotional Strategy:** Products with high profit margins but low market penetration are prioritized for micro-discounts to unlock "trapped" gross profit dollars without requiring new product development.



## Technical Methodology

* 
**Data Visualization:** The analysis utilizes Pareto charts to map vendor contributions, box plots for distribution analysis, and scatter plots to correlate sales performance with profit margins.


* 
**Statistical Rigor:** Statistical validation is applied to margin data, ensuring that vendor tiering and budgeting decisions are based on mathematical certainty rather than intuition.
